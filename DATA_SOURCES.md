# Data sources and attribution

Image distributions used in the corrected study:

- PlantVillage potato images: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arjuntejaswi/plant-village
- Field potato images, distributed from the Shabrina et al. collection: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/warcoder/potato-leaf-disease-dataset

The eight source photographs in `samples/` originate from the public PlantVillage repository. Their source URLs, original filenames, image IDs, hashes and published leaf identities are recorded in `figures/sample_provenance.json`. Original image pixels are unchanged. Source repository and metadata revision: https://github.com/spMohanty/PlantVillage-Dataset/tree/7f7ecc7e1eaca78107e3affe7cb5abd9427e139a.

Reference: Mohanty SP, Hughes DP and Salathé M (2016). Using Deep Learning for Image-Based Plant Disease Detection. Frontiers in Plant Science 7:1419. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419

Reference: Shabrina NH et al. (2024). A novel dataset of potato leaf disease in uncontrolled environment. Data in Brief 52:109955. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109955

Source datasets and third-party metadata retain their respective terms and attribution requirements. This deposit does not claim new ownership of those materials or assign a new blanket license to them. The repository's earlier educational-use statement is preserved with the historical material; no new license is inferred for all coauthors or third-party assets.
